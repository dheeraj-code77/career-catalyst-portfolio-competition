WHAT IS A PORTFOLIO BUILDING COMPETITION?

A showcase of what you know and what you can do.

A portfolio is more than a collection of documents. It brings together your learning, skills, projects, achievements and practical work.

Build and present your own portfolio to show:

  • What you have learned.
  • Which skills you have developed.
  • What you have created or accomplished with those skills.
  • Your projects, practical work and achievements.
  • Evidence of your learning and progress — your Proof of Work.

THE IDEA IS SIMPLE

Don’t just tell us what you know. Show us what you have learned and what you have done with it.

Build your portfolio, present your work and make your learning visible.

THE IDEA BEHIND THE CHALLENGE CAREER CATALYST

More than a resume.
A story of your work.

Career Catalyst is a portfolio-building competition that encourages you to bring your projects, skills, achievements and professional presence together in one clear place. The goal is simple: make it easier for others to understand what you can do.

01 / BUILD

Bring your work together

Showcase meaningful projects, achievements, certificates and skills instead of leaving them scattered across platforms.

02 / PRESENT

Make it easy to explore

Organise your work into a professional portfolio PDF, supported by relevant links such as LinkedIn and GitHub.

03 / GROW

Get recognised

Entries are evaluated on a 100-point scale, with the top three participants receiving public recognition and a dedicated shout-out.
